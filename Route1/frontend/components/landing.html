Minnesota food rescue marketplace

Good food shouldn't go to waste.
Let's get the surplus harvest to neighbors who need it.

Route 1 Pathways connects local farmers, food shelves, and neighbors so surplus crops feed people — not landfills.

How it works

One route, from field to table

🌱

Farmers list what they grow

Post your crops, set a price or mark them donation-ready, and say whether you can help with transport.

🥫

Organizations open opportunities

Food shelves, grocers, and restaurants turn surplus into donation opportunities neighbors can fund.

🤝

Neighbors fund the harvest

Chip in on a crop — on your own or together with others — and earn rewards as you give.

Why it matters

Plenty of food. Not enough of it reaching people.

About a third of the food grown in this country never gets eaten — while millions of families a few miles away can't count on their next meal. The food already exists. What's missing is the path between them.

That path is what Route 1 builds: a direct line from a Minnesota farm with extra to a neighbor who needs it.
