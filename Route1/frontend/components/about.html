About Route 1 Pathways

A Minnesota food rescue marketplace

Every season, good food goes unharvested or unsold while families across Minnesota go without. Route 1 Pathways exists to close that gap — a simple, direct path from a farm with surplus to the neighbors who need it.

🌱 For farmers

List what you grow — set a price, mark crops donation-ready, and note if you can help with transport. Reach buyers and food shelves directly.

🥫 For organizations

Food shelves, grocers, and restaurants turn surplus into donation opportunities the community can fund — and connect with the farmers who grow it.

🤝 For neighbors

Fund a crop on your own or together with others, support local growers, and earn rewards as you help feed your community.

Why we built this

Roughly a third of the food grown in this country never gets eaten, while more than 40 million Americans face food insecurity. The food already exists — what's usually missing is the path between the people who have extra and the people who need it. Route 1 Pathways is built, with our community partners, to be that path: practical, local, and built for the Minnesota farmers, food shelves, and families who use it.
